Frequently Asked Repair Questions

Everything you should know before visiting our workbench.

Do I have to share my phone lock screen passcode? ▼

No! We do not require your passcode. For display and touch testing, we use standard emergency dialer diagnostic codes or you can test and unlock the phone yourself before paying. Your photos, WhatsApp, and private banking data remain 100% untouched.

How does the 90-Day Store Warranty work? ▼

Every screen and battery replacement comes with an official printed invoice stamped by OM SAI MOBILE. If your replacement touch digitizer shows phantom touch, color bleed, or battery fails to hold charge within 90 days (180 days for batteries), we replace it free of charge on spot without courier delays.

Can I watch the repair while sitting at your store? ▼

Yes, absolutely. Our service desk features an open glass view. You are welcome to sit directly across from the technician or watch on our counter screen as your phone is disassembled and serviced.
